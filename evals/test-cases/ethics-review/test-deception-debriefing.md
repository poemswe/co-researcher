# Test Case: Deception Without Debriefing (Ethics Expert)

## Metadata
- **Capability**: ethics-review
- **Implementation Skill**: ethics-review
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Area**: Deception & Participant Welfare

## Rubric Profile
- **Primary**: design-quality (70%)
- **Secondary**: analytical-quality (20%)
- **Tertiary**: output-structure (10%)

## Task Prompt

```
Evaluate the following study for ethical risks before we submit it:

**Project**: "Effects of Negative Feedback on Persistence"
**Design**: We will recruit 400 participants on an online crowdsourcing platform. Each writes a short essay, then receives fabricated feedback saying their essay ranked in the bottom 5% of all submissions, regardless of quality. We measure how long they keep working on a follow-up task. To avoid contaminating the participant pool, we will not tell anyone afterwards that the feedback was fake. Participants confirm they are adults with a checkbox; we do not verify age. Pay is $0.50 for an expected 20 minutes. Because the study is online and anonymous, we plan to claim exemption from IRB review.
```

## Expected Behaviors

### Must Include
- [ ] Identify Deception Without Debriefing: State that deceiving participants requires justification and a debriefing that reveals the deception, and that omitting debriefing is a serious problem.
- [ ] Identify Psychological Harm: Note that false bottom-5% feedback can cause distress, so the study is not plainly minimal risk.
- [ ] Identify Inadequate Compensation: Note that $0.50 for 20 minutes is far below a fair wage.
- [ ] Reject the Exemption Claim: Explain that online or anonymous data collection does not by itself make a deception study exempt, and that IRB review is required.

### Should Include
- [ ] Concern that an unverified age checkbox may let minors participate
- [ ] Discussion of whether the research question could be answered without deception
- [ ] A concrete debriefing plan, including the option to withdraw data
- [ ] Reference to an ethics framework such as the Belmont Report or the APA Ethics Code

### Should Not Include
- [ ] Approving the design as written
- [ ] Treating pool contamination as sufficient reason to skip debriefing
- [ ] Claiming the study qualifies as exempt because it is online
- [ ] Ignoring the compensation problem

## Evaluation Criteria

### Research Quality
- Accuracy about deception, debriefing and review requirements

### Reasoning Quality (Primary)
- Depth of the risk analysis
- Quality of the proposed alternatives

### Output Structure
- Risks organized by severity
- Clear final recommendation

## Passing Threshold
- Overall Score: ≥ 75/100
